# Gudruts design system

Everything an assistant (ChatGPT, Claude or similar) needs to make on-brand Gudruts work — Instagram posts, website sections, emails, new Gudu outfits — with no other context.

## How to use it

1. Get the files: click **Code → Download ZIP** on this page and unzip it.
2. Open a new chat and paste or attach `GUDRUTS-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` (or the raw link: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TEJASVISJAIN/gudruts-design-system/main/GUDRUTS-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).
3. Attach the logo (`brand/wordmark.png`, or `brand/wordmark-cream.png` for dark backgrounds), the Gudu poses you need (`gudu/png/`) and 2–3 screenshots from `reference/`.
4. Ask for what you need. Section 15 of the document has ready-made prompts.

## What's inside

| Folder | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `GUDRUTS-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` | The full rulebook: brand rules, voice, colours, type, Gudu, components, channel recipes, calendar, checklist |
| `brand/` | Logos, roundel, avatars, ingredient art |
| `gudu/` | Official Gudu poses and outfits as SVG and transparent PNG |
| `reference/` | Screenshots of the site, email, share card and social posts |
| `source/` | Tokens, Gudu components and email templates from the live site |

Regenerated from the live site on 15 Nov 2026. When the site changes, update the document and re-export the Gudu files from https://gudruts.com/#/styleguide.
