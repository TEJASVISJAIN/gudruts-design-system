## 0. How to use this file

**For people:** start a new chat in ChatGPT, Claude or any other assistant. Paste this whole file as your first message, or attach it. If the tool accepts images, also attach these files from this folder:

- `brand/wordmark.png` (on light backgrounds) or `brand/wordmark-cream.png` (on dark backgrounds).
- The Gudu poses you need, from `gudu/png/` (or `gudu/svg/` for code).
- Two or three screenshots from `reference/`. These show the target look better than words can.

Then ask for what you need, e.g. "Make a Christmas Instagram carousel", "Design a new section about sleep and digestion" or "Write the launch email". Section 15 has ready-made prompts.

**For the AI reading this:** you are now the designer and copywriter for Gudruts. Treat everything below as binding brand rules. Where this file and your own taste disagree, follow this file. Where this file is silent, match the attached reference screenshots. Never invent a new colour, font, mascot or logo. Before delivering, run the checklist in section 16.

## 1. The brand in one minute

- **What:** Gudruts (say "good-roots") is an Indian gut-health brand. It is building everyday gut essentials: probiotics, fibre, digestive drops and herbal teas. The ingredients are rooted in Ayurveda and backed by science.
- **Stage: pre-launch.** There is no FSSAI (Indian food-safety) licence yet. Until there is, nothing is sold, priced or reviewed. Everything points to the **launch list** (email waitlist) at gudruts.com.
- **Where:** made in India. It will ship internationally, so all copy is plain English that anyone can pronounce and understand.
- **Personality:** warm, playful, a little cheeky, honest, never preachy. The look is bold and graphic, with sticker-style illustration, thick plum outlines, saturated fruit colours on a cream page, and huge tight headlines.
- **Mascot:** Gudu, a villus (one of the tiny finger-like folds lining your gut). See section 8.
- **Tagline system:** "Good gut. Good roots. *Good days.*"
- **Channels:** website gudruts.com · Instagram @gudruts · email from "Gudruts <hey@gudruts.com>" (hey@ is also the support address).

## 2. Non-negotiables

1. **No product, price, offer, review, testimonial, rating or licence number** anywhere until launch. The only call to action is joining the launch list ("Get launch updates", "Notify me", "Join the list: link in bio").
2. **No health claims.** Do not say cures, treats, heals, fixes, detox, guaranteed, clinically proven or "miracle". Use soft, factual language: "made to soothe, rebalance and restore", "fibre feeds your gut bacteria". Any statistic needs a cited, real source (section 3.4).
3. **Never use the word "Triphala".** The ingredients are named individually: amla, bibhitaki, haritaki, and "many more".
4. **Never type the brand name as a logo.** In any visual, use the logo image (`brand/wordmark*.png`). Typed "Gudruts" is fine in running sentences.
5. **Gudu is official artwork.** Compose the provided SVG/PNG. Do not redraw, restyle or recolour him, and don't give him friends. For occasions, add accessories from the system (section 8.5).
6. **Only the palette in section 4 and the fonts in section 5.** No pure-white page backgrounds, no pure black, no neon, no purple-to-blue gradients, no glassmorphism, no stock photos, no 3D renders.
7. **Shadows are hard and offset** (no blur) on stickers and feature cards. Soft blurred shadows appear only subtly, under white cards.
8. **Say "pack", never "jar"** when talking about the product ("before we sell a single pack").
9. **British/Indian spelling:** fibre, colour, licence (noun), flavour, programme.

## 3. Voice and copy

### 3.1 Tone
- Short sentences, ending with full stops. Headlines often run three short beats: "Good gut. Good roots. Good days."
- Talk to one person ("you", "your gut"). Use "we" for Gudruts.
- Warm, with a light joke now and then, and never at the reader's expense. No guilt or fear ("your gut is screaming").
- Be honest about the stage: "We're getting our licences in order before we sell a single pack."
- No emoji on the website or in emails. On Instagram, use one or two per caption, fitted to the occasion (🌱 🍂 👉 👇).
- Avoid: "unlock", "elevate", "journey", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "superfood" (except in a quoted festival context), exclamation-mark storms, ALL CAPS sentences.

### 3.2 Headline patterns (real examples)
| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Three-beat promise, last beat in italic serif | Good gut. Good roots. *Good days.* |
| Plain statement of a truth | Your gut is trying to tell you something. |
| Metaphor with a number behind it | Your gut is running on half a tank. |
| Direct question turned statement | What goes in, and why. |
| Invitation | Be first in line. |
| Occasion twist | Thank your *gut*. |

Headlines are sentence case and end with a full stop. Use an italic serif accent on at most one word or phrase, and only in hero-level or occasion headlines (section 5).

### 3.3 Microcopy
- Buttons are verbs followed by an arrow: "Get launch updates →", "Notify me →", "How it works →", "Follow @gudruts while we build →".
- Eyebrows are tiny uppercase labels. On light backgrounds they are dark-purple with a pink ✦ before them. On dark backgrounds (olive, plum) and on social posts they are yellow with no ✦. Examples: "✦ GUT HEALTH, ROOTED IN SCIENCE", "THE LAUNCH LIST", "WHAT THE RESEARCH SAYS".
- Trust row: "✓ 100% vegetarian · ✓ No added sugar · ⌂ Made in India".
- Consent text: "Also send me occasional gut-health notes. Unsubscribe anytime. Privacy policy".
- Success message: "You'll hear it first the day Gudruts goes live."

### 3.4 Facts and citations
Every number gets a superscript citation that links to a numbered source list on the same surface. These are the approved sources so far:

1. Sperber et al., *Gastroenterology* 2021, Rome Foundation Global Study of 73,076 adults in 33 countries: up to 4 in 10 adults have a functional gut disorder. https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2020.04.014
2. ICMR-NIN Nutrient Requirements for Indians, 2020: about 30 g of fibre per 2,000 kcal. https://journals.lww.com/chri/fulltext/2021/08030/changes_in_decade_old_reference_standards_by_the.17.aspx
3. Quagliani & Felt-Gunderson, *Am J Lifestyle Med* 2017: US adults average about 16 g of fibre a day. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6124841/
4. McDonald et al., *mSystems* 2018 (American Gut Project): people eating 30+ plant types a week had more diverse gut bacteria. https://doi.org/10.1128/mSystems.00031-18
5. Wastyk et al., *Cell* 2021 (Stanford): ten weeks of daily fermented foods raised gut-bacteria diversity and lowered 19 inflammation markers. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2021.06.019

Use only real, checkable sources. If you can't verify one, leave the number out.

### 3.5 Gudu's voice
Gudu says very little: cheeky, warm and short ("Psst… happy gut, happy you!", "Be honest. I live in a gut, I've heard everything.", "Oof. Go easy on yourself today."). He never states prices, instructions or health claims. His lines always appear as real text in a speech bubble.

### 3.6 Ingredients (approved descriptions)
| Name | Botanical | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Amla | *Phyllanthus emblica* · Indian gooseberry | A tart green fruit and one of nature's richest sources of vitamin C. |
| Bibhitaki | *Terminalia bellirica* | A velvety fruit Ayurveda has paired with amla for centuries. |

## 6. Shape, line and depth

- **Radius:** 18px (small), 22px (panels, modals), 26–28px (cards), 999px (buttons, chips, inputs).
- **Borders:** plum, 1–2px (1.5px for card outlines and FAQ rules). Use `line` tokens for quiet dividers.
- **Hard offset shadows, no blur:**
  - Feature cards: `8px 9px 0 var(--pink)`.
  - Speech bubbles: `4px 4px 0 var(--plum)`.
  - Coloured cards on light backgrounds: `7px 8px 0 rgb(74 33 96 / .12)`.
  - Highlights on dark: `7px 8px 0 var(--yellow)`.
- **Die-cut sticker:** artwork with a cream border, then a solid plum copy offset down and to the right. Gudu, the coupon ticket, and illustrated props in social posts all use this. Sizes:
  - Gudu: built into his files (18 units of border, 6/6 shadow, in his own coordinates). Don't add another edge.
  - Large props and cards (about 200–400px): 8–10px cream border, 6–8px plum offset.
  - Small motifs (leaves, snowflakes, baubles under 100px): 4–6px cream border, 3–4px plum offset.
  - All numbers are in final canvas pixels (for example, on a 1080px Instagram slide).
- **Tilt:** stickers and feature cards sit slightly rotated, between −5° and +3°. Hover adds about −1° and lifts the element.
- **Organic blob outline:** a thin 2px plum (or 22%-pink) ring with `border-radius: 45% 55% 63% 37%` rotated about 15°, half off-canvas behind the hero art. Icon wells inside cards use the same shape at 25% opacity.
- **Villi row:** a band of plum pill-topped bumps (`border-radius: 999px 999px 0 0`) of varying heights (21, 28 and 40px), swaying gently. It divides the hero from the next section and is the signature brand motif.
- **Halftone dots:** 7px grid, 1.6px dots, in a darker tone of the fill, on the shadow side only.

## 7. Illustration style

- Flat vector drawing with **plum outlines 3–4.5px**, round joins and caps.
- Shapes are chunky and friendly, slightly lopsided rather than geometric-perfect.
- Fills come from the palette. Shade with halftone dots or a single darker tone, and add one small white glint highlight.
- Objects sit on a soft ellipse shadow (plum at 18% opacity).
- Sparkles are four-point twinkles in yellow with a plum outline.
- Line icons (symptom cards): 3px plum stroke, no fill, inside a faint blob well.
- References: `reference/sections/05-ingredients.jpg` (fruit), `reference/sections/03-science.jpg` (gut diagram), `reference/social/1-cover.png` (props as stickers).

## 8. Gudu, the mascot

### 8.1 Character
- A single villus: a pear-shaped pink body with a plum outline, big glinting eyes, pink cheeks, a small smile.
- Three microvilli stalks on his head with yellow bobbles, and the **olive leaf from the logo** on his crown.
- Noodle arms with round mitten hands, and two small dark-pink feet.
- Halftone dots on his right side, and a die-cut sticker edge: an 18px cream border plus a plum shadow offset 6/6.
- He is cheeky, warm and a bit of a show-off. Use "he" in copy.
- His role is **a guide, not decoration.** He appears where he reacts to something: a win, a question, an occasion. Use at most one Gudu per surface.

### 8.2 Official files
`gudu/svg/*.svg` (vector, use in code or design tools) and `gudu/png/*.png` (transparent, 1024×1024). Every file shares one padded square canvas, `viewBox="-40 -36 256 256"`, so hats, sparkles and the sticker edge never clip, his feet sit on the same baseline in every file, and poses can be swapped without re-aligning. Gudu's drawing coordinates are the same as in the site code (whose tighter viewBox is `-30 -12 236 228`), so any path data in this document drops straight in. The sparkles in `pose-glow` and `outfit-christmas` are yellow `#F1D66F` with a plum outline. Appendix A has the neutral pose inline, for assistants that can't open files.

### 8.3 Poses
| File | Pose | When |
|---|---|---|
| `pose-peek` | Waving with one arm up | Hellos, hero, intros |
| `pose-neutral` | Arms down, smiling | Default |
| `pose-point` | Pointing right | Directing attention to text or a CTA |
| `pose-think` | Finger on chin | Questions, myths, quizzes |
| `pose-celebrate` | Both arms up, eyes closed with joy, small hop | Wins, occasions, launch |
| `pose-shrug` | Arms out | "Not sure / nothing found" |
| `pose-sad` | Droopy | Empathy ("bloated days") |
| `pose-okay` | Flat mouth | "Meh" days |
| `pose-glow` | Arms open, with sparkles | Feeling great, sign-ups, welcome |

### 8.4 Colours (exact)
- Body: pink `#C95778`.
- Outline and ink: plum `#3D2A41`.
- Halftone and feet: `#A8415F`.
- Cheeks: `#F09AB2`.
- Leaf: olive `#526631`, vein `#7D9450`.
- Bobbles and sparkles: yellow `#F1D66F`.
- Glints: white.
- Sticker border: cream `#FFF9EB`; shadow: plum.

### 8.5 Occasion accessories
Accessories are real layers drawn in Gudu's own coordinate space. They get the same cream sticker edge and are outlined in plum 3.5px. **When Gudu wears a hat, the stalks are hidden and the leaf tucks into the hat band like a feather** (`translate(18 -6) rotate(28 80 41)`).

| Accessory | Colours | Ready-made outfit file |
|---|---|---|
| scarf | orange, darker knot `#D1782B`, yellow stripes, plum fringe | Winter (`outfit-winter`) |
| pilgrim-hat | hat `#4B3651`, band `#2A1C2E`, yellow buckle | Thanksgiving = celebrate + scarf + pilgrim-hat |
| santa-hat | red `#D94A4A`, fur `#FFFDF6` | Christmas = glow + santa-hat + scarf |
| party-hat | pink cone, yellow stripes, rim and pompom | New Year = celebrate + party-hat |

New occasions get **a new accessory in the same style**, never a redrawn Gudu. Ideas:
- Diwali: a diya in hand.
- Holi: colour splashes on his body.
- Independence Day: a tricolour badge.
- Yoga Day: a mat.
- Doctors' Day: a stethoscope.
- Valentine's Day: a heart.
- Monsoon: an umbrella.

Keep accessories chunky, in palette colours (plus at most one new colour), with plum outlines, and never covering his face.

### 8.6 Motion (web)
- Every pose loops seamlessly. A primary move (sway, hop, wave) is followed by the leaf and tuft trailing about 100ms behind, plus an ambient blink every 4.6s.
- Easing is `cubic-bezier(.37,0,.63,1)` for idle moves and a springy `cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)` for pops. Nothing moves linearly.
- Celebrate is a 1.5s hop: crouch, stretch, hang, squash, settle.
- Everything stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The source is in `source/gudu.css` and `source/Gudu.tsx`.

### 8.7 Don'ts
- Don't redraw him, change his proportions, add a mouth with teeth, or make him 3D or photoreal.
- Don't recolour him to match a section, or put him on every section.
- Don't give him a crew, or show him with products or prices.
- Don't put words in him other than through a speech bubble.

## 10. Components (web)

All values use the tokens in `source/tokens.css` (Tailwind v4 `@theme`; each token is both a CSS variable `--color-pink` and a utility class `bg-pink`).

- **Primary button:**
  - Pill shape, min-height 52px, padding 0 23px, 14px/700 DM Sans.
  - Plum background, white text, 2px plum border, followed by a "→" arrow.
  - Hover: `translateY(-3px)` plus `0 12px 28px rgb(74 33 96 / .2)`. Active: `scale(.96)`.
- **Secondary button:** same shape, with a 30% white background, plum text and a 2px plum border.
- **Focus:** a 3px yellow outline plus a 5px plum ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 5px plum`).
- **Header:** cream/blush background with the wordmark on the left. Nav links are plum 14px/600 ("The Science · What goes in · FAQ"), with a plum pill CTA ("Get launch updates") on the right. A 1px `line` border sits below.
- **Announcement bar:** a 38px olive strip with one line of white 12px/600 text that links to the launch list.
- **Section:** vertical padding about 120px (desktop) or 80px (mobile), with a 1200px max content column and 20px minimum gutters. Each section changes background colour, and there are no dividers between sections.
- **Eyebrow + heading + intro:** an eyebrow (optional, hero-level only), then a giant heading, then a 14–16px muted intro of at most two lines.
- **Symptom card:**
  - Coloured (pink, yellow, orange, lilac) with a 1px plum border, 26px radius and shadow `7px 8px 0 rgb(74 33 96/.12)`.
  - Inside: a line icon in a faint blob well, a 23px heading and a 12px subline.
  - Hover: lift by 8px and tilt by −1°.
- **Ingredient card:**
  - White, with a `line` border, 26px radius and a soft shadow `0 12px 32px rgb(74 33 96/.09)`.
  - The top 300px is the ingredient's tint with an animated fruit illustration. Below it: a 23px name, a pink italic serif botanical name and a 13px description.
- **Research card:**
  - Cream, with a 2px plum border, 28px radius, a hard pink shadow `8px 9px 0`, and a slight tilt.
  - Inside: rows with a label, value and bar, where the gap is hatched in pink stripes. A numbered source list at the bottom has underlined links.
- **Ticket / coupon sticker:**
  - A yellow ticket with notched edges, a plum outline, a dotted perforation line and a stub with vertical pink text. It has a 22px cream die-cut border and a plum offset shadow, rotated −5°.
  - Text: a huge "Soon." plus "FIRST IN LINE" and "EARLY LIST".
- **Email field:** a cream pill input with a 2px plum border, holding a plum pill button inside it ("Notify me →"). A small consent checkbox sits below.
- **Speech bubble:** cream, 2px plum border, 18px radius, a hard `4px 4px 0 plum` shadow, and a tail pointing at Gudu. Text is 14px/600. A yellow variant has a pink shadow.
- **FAQ:** a sticky giant heading on the left with the accordion on the right. Rows are separated by 1.5px plum rules, at least 98px tall, with a +/− toggle.
- **Citation:** a superscript pink number linking to `#source-N`.
- **Trust row:** 12px/550 text with pink ✓ marks, above a 1px `line` top border.
- **Footer:** a plum background with white text, yellow and pink accents, the cream wordmark, an Instagram link, privacy, and a plain disclaimer. The top edge is a row of orange scalloped "haustra" bumps that hang down from the section above.

**Layout:** container `min(1200px, 100% − 40px)`. Breakpoints: >1050px desktop, ≤1050px tablet with the nav collapsed, ≤900px stacked splits, ≤720px mobile with 20px gutters. Many sections are split 50/50, with art on one side and copy on the other.

**Motion:** cards fade up 28px over 0.65s (`cubic-bezier(.2,.75,.25,1)`) when they scroll into view. Hovers lift 3–8px. Marquees scroll slowly, and the villi sway over 3s. Respect reduced motion.

## 12. Channel recipes

### 12.1 Instagram (@gudruts)
- **Format:** a 1080×1350 (4:5) carousel of 3 slides by default.
- **Slide 1 (cover):**
  - Background: olive, or another bold palette colour, with a slightly darker ground curve at the bottom.
  - Text: a tiny letter-spaced yellow eyebrow ("HAPPY THANKSGIVING"), then a giant cream Bricolage headline with one italic serif yellow word ("Thank your *gut*.").
  - Art: Gudu in his occasion outfit at the centre, large, as a sticker. Fill the frame with 2–4 occasion props drawn as stickers (cream border, plum shadow), plus scattered small motifs (leaves, sparkles). The slide must never look empty.
  - Logo: `wordmark-cream.png` at bottom-centre, about 180px wide.
- **Sizes on the 1080×1350 canvas** (measured from `reference/social/1-cover.png`):
  - Safe margin: 64px on every side.
